# Assets Guide

## Screenshot Placement
Screenshots and visual guides should be placed in the `/assets/screenshots` directory. Ensure that all images are named clearly and descriptively.

## Important Settings Annotations
When documenting important settings in the visual guides:
- Use clear arrows or highlights to indicate the settings.
- Include concise text annotations to explain the significance of these settings.
- Ensure the annotations are easily readable and not crowded with other information.

By following these guidelines, we maintain a standard for clarity and usability for all contributors and users.
