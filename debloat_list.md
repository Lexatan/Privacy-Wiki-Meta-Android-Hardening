# Debloat List for Poco/Xiaomi Devices

The following packages are commonly regarded as tracking or bloatware that can be safely removed from Poco/Xiaomi devices. You can use either Canta or ADB to uninstall these packages.

### Tracking/Bloatware Packages
- com.miui.analytics
- com.miui.bugreport
- com.miui.securitycenter
- com.miui.cloudservice
- com.samsung.android.providers.media
- com.samsung.android.providers.downloads
- com.miui.videoplayer
- com.miui.player
- com.xiaomi.mipush.sdk
- com.xiaomi.pushservice

### Notes
- Always ensure you know what the package does before removal.
- It's advisable to make a backup before debloating your device.
